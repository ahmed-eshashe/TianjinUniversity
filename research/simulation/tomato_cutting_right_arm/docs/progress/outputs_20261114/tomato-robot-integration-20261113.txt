after 1 s, so its overall result remains failed. A longer unchanged-law settlement
probe resolved that condition. The final controller permits up to 20 s settlement
and requires 0.5 s continuously below the existing speed/support threshold.

positive clearance on the preceding step. The prior native contact envelope
was 4 mm combined; the corrected envelope is 0.5 mm combined, with zero rest offset
and the existing 1 mm geometric clearance stop. Blade/board geometry and tomato
laws were not changed. No collision pair or physical stop was disabled to pass.

Force convergence is not established. Peak-load differences across exchange
trials were about 5–8%, and their native contact settings were not all identical.

The material is explicitly approximate and uncalibrated. Cosmetic skin, calyx
and cut flesh do not establish anatomical or experimental material realism.
