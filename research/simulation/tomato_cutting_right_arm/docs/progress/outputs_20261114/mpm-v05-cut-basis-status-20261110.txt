CUT-AWARE MECHANICS v05 — RESTRICTED BLOCK PROOF COMPLETE; OFF-GRID CUTS BLOCKED

Outcome
The separate candidate now preserves intact material ahead of a partial incision and allows two fully divided pieces to move independently, including after a rigid translation/rotation. Loaded state transfer, energy consistency, timestep refinement and complete unit-fixture checkpoint/history checks pass. These are prescribed-topology component tests on a 12 mm block, NOT a blade-generated physical cut, a finished tomato, a Gate 2/3 pass, or an RL-ready solver. The existing Isaac/DiSECt setup is unchanged.

The off-grid negative control correctly stops: moving the full division plane by 0.3 mm creates 36 unresolved scalar basis modes. Fixed material samples miss an entire thin support region. The smallest demonstrated next repair is cut-aware numerical integration, not changing the tomato material or hiding the modes with added mass. A diagnostic integration calculation removes the rank loss, but its altered inertia/energy sampling requires fresh-state qualification. No such change has been adopted in this version.

Implemented representation
v05 retains the v04 reference-coordinate quadratic B-spline mechanics, consistent mass and velocity-Verlet update. Exact knot insertion and local enrichment provide a discontinuity across one reference-X-normal plane. An enrichment activates only when its complete transverse support lies within the supplied finite rectangle. Original basis columns remain, so introducing a partial incision does not soften the untouched bridge by replacing its basis globally. Newly added coefficients start at zero; old coefficients and the existing x/v/F field persist. The cut coordinates are attached to the material reference, not a stationary world obstacle. This is a CPU block proof, not an implementation of official CRESSim or BladeMaster.

Blocking result and independent diagnosis
A full plane at X=+0.3 mm needs 324 scalar basis coefficients but the current material-value matrix has rank 288. The candidate rejects it before changing state. An explicit null witness has zero sampled displacement AND zero sampled gradient, yet independent integration sees a maximum continuous strain gradient of 0.002199 and positive strain energy 2.323e-10 J. Thus this is a real sampling blind region, not a harmless round-off mode.
A diagnostic using three Gauss points per X subcell and unchanged transverse sampling gives 960 integration points, preserves 0.001728 kg total mass, and has full rank 324 with condition about 7740.8 in the full split-basis coordinates. This is not a dynamic validation and is not the enriched-coordinate condition number. It changes the base mass matrix by 6.105% in Frobenius norm. For one saved loaded v04 state it changes calculated total energy by +0.2003%, from 3.16210e-8 to 3.16844e-8 J, despite unchanged geometry/strain fields. It therefore cannot be spliced into the old trajectory and called an exact energy-preserving restart. See RECOVERY_PROPOSAL.txt for a bounded, fresh-state validation proposal.
