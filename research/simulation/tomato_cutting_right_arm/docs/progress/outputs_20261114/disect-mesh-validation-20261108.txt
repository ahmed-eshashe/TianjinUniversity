PRISM REPAIRS AND VALIDATION
The boundary polygon was triangulated repeatedly inside its edge-collection loop. Moving triangulation after the loop removes the duplicate faces. Coincident plane/vertex intersections also generated roundoff-scale zero-measure triangles. A documented relative floating-point area test skips those surface triangles during construction. It does not remove cutting springs or material connections. Both patch variants and failed intermediate mesh audit are retained.
After the final patch: 1223 combined cut faces, 1058 virtual cut faces, 529 on each side. All audited face arrays have zero degenerate faces (area threshold 1e-15 m2) and zero duplicate unoriented triangles. Minimum combined face area is 4.77e-9 m2, far above that threshold.
Independent Shapely polygon unions verify each cut side covers the full 0.005 m2 prism section: uncovered area 0, outside area 8.72e-20 m2 and overlap area ~1.73e-18 m2. Sides have the same geometric triangles with opposite normals. Particle positions, tetrahedra, original exterior triangles, edge interpolation indices/coordinates and cutting spring indices are bitwise unchanged against v01. Material/contact laws and compiled force kernel binary were not changed.
Two complete 50,000-step / 5-second forward runs exit normally. Setup: 8.730 and 9.054 s; stepping: 8.540 and 8.744 s; 1.708 and 1.749 wall seconds per simulated second. All sampled states finite; final minimum signed tet volume ratios ~0.99417, no final inversion. Max repeated node difference 0.264 mm. Two cutting springs retain nonzero stiffness in each run: this example does not demonstrate complete detachment. No gradient/backward qualification. Torch peak allocations are narrower than whole GPU process memory; no new total GPU memory comparison is claimed.
