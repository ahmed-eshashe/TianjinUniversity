ASTRA REVIEW: LARGE EFFORT DISCREPANCY IDENTIFIED — 2 November 2026

Result
The approximately11.2Nm discrepancy is explained by a missing descendant-feedforward contribution in the CPU TGS joint-force readout path used by this project. An additive OFFLINE observation reconstruction reproduces that contribution from physical parameters and commanded joint efforts, without fitting a coefficient or altering simulation physics. The original full moving force/work qualification remains open because residual precision still matters at the project's very small contact-work scale.

Why Sol's earlier investigation stalled
The measured projected effort and projected incoming wrench are two views of the same backend result. Their agreement verifies mapping, but cannot establish that the backend result contains every contribution. The discrepancy is not explained by local rotor inertia or a change of joint moment origin. Looking only at one joint misses a contribution propagated from its descendants.

Source-backed mechanism
The installed tensor extension is110.3.2. The public matching release-family source110.3-omni-and-physx-5.9.1 explicitly passes a null joint-force pointer when external forces are applied on every TGS iteration. Consequently the cached articulated internal bias omits the descendant feedforward torque contribution. Feedforward still acts in the TGS substep force path. The incoming-joint-force query subsequently combines the cached articulated inertia/bias with the solver's accelerations and impulses. The omitted bias is not restored by that query.

Reconstruction
Use a backward articulated-body recursion in physical body order. At each revolute joint, U=IA*S and d=S^T*IA*S+A, where A is its measured armature. Propagate the reduced inertia IA-U*U^T/d and the incremental feedforward bias b+U*(tau_ff-S^T*b)/d into the parent, with the correct spatial-force transform. Starting from zero local bias isolates only the contribution of explicit joint efforts. At a given link, the accumulated bias is the contribution of its descendants; leaf correction is zero. Transport this spatial bias into the reported child-joint frame and ADD it to the raw incoming wrench. The operation changes observation data only.

Projection gives the reconstructed active-axis joint load. Local A*delta_qdot/dt is accounted separately when comparing against total known generalized input in the zero-drive controls. This comparison still requires the actual passive-constraint/limit/friction conditions; it is not a universal motor-torque sensor formula. Current runtime joint friction values captured in the diagnostic are zero. The reconstruction is limited to this evidenced fixed-base, revolute/fixed tree with CPU TGS and external forces every iteration. It must not be applied blindly to PGS, GPU backends, changed actuator paths or other articulation types.
