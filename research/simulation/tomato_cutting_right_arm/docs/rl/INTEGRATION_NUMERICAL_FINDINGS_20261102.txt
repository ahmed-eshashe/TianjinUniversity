Recommended work fixture after stable target mechanics
Use a separately recorded passive zero-gravity robot fixture with every drive,
joint friction and link damping explicitly read back, a fixed spring-supported
elastic coupon and a resolvable low load. Keep the real mount and body inertias.
Measure contact work from actual COM twists/poses, generalized contact torque
from the actual COM Jacobian, and kinetic energy from the actual joint-space
mass matrix. Reconcile joint- and body-based energy conventions before claiming
a balance; do not guess inertia frames. Account for armature explicitly or set
it to a declared diagnostic zero. Fixed-base/fixture work is zero only after
actual pose invariance is checked. Repeat at smaller coupling steps.
For driven work, use observed applied actuator effort or an explicit known
bounded torque fixture. An implicit-drive effort estimate is not independent
actuator work. A separate actual-drive/controller qualification is required
before its budget is used for the cutting controller.
