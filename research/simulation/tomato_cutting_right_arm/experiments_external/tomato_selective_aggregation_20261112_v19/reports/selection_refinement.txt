Targeted numerical refinement, 2026-11-12

The two initial selectors are rejected as global settings: the 130 micrometre corner fixture has a 29.54 mm/s physical-vertex velocity discrepancy under 1e-8, and the 10 micrometre fixture has a 7.66 mm/s discrepancy under both 1e-8 and 1e-12. Force/position/energy pass, but the 5 mm/s velocity limit remains in force. The 1 micrometre fixture passes with 2.72 mm/s discrepancy. These outcomes are preserved in response.json/response.partial.json.

A final targeted refinement retains the 10 micrometre modes as well by reducing the numerical support selector to 1e-16. This only restores additional original deformation degrees of freedom and leaves all physical material, inertia, force/energy laws and acceptance criteria unchanged. It is a parameter-convergence correction within the user's authorized deformation-preserving work; no new backend or physical model is introduced. The initially declared two-selector budget is amended explicitly rather than describing this as a predeclared third candidate. The original two-hour campaign limit still applies. No fourth threshold will be tried.

Previously accepted cases may be reused only if their extension, master IDs and reduced mass are demonstrated identical under the final selector. New/different operators require their own comparison. With an identity extension the algorithm is exactly the preserved v17 formulation; original GPU dynamics may be used to establish timestep convergence without waiting on an identical CPU loop. This is not qualification of a new aggregate GPU implementation.
