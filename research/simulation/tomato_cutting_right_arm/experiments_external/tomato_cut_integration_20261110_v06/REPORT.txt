Outcome

Numerical change
Mechanical integration now covers every reference cell and its cut-created subcells. It no longer assumes the original observation points resolve all new basis functions. The primary rule has five Gauss points per axis per cell; order seven checks nonlinear strain-energy convergence. Polynomial mass agrees with independent order-three integration. The same continuous displacement gives motion, strain, energy and force. Exact basis insertion copies the loaded field, while any integration-related energy change must remain inside the original 1e-12 J / 1e-8 relative transfer tolerance. Contact/fracture has not been added. See physical_model.txt for the equations and restrictions.
