PROPOSED NEXT SCOPE — CUT-AWARE INTEGRATION, FRESH-STATE REVALIDATION
Prepared 10 November 2026. Not adopted or running.

Decision
Validate one numerical integration revision in a new isolated v06 directory. The reviewed roadmap requires a revised scope after a failed physical/representation gate; v05 demonstrates that a cut shifted only 0.3 mm is unsupported by the fixed material samples. Do not spend another long tomato run on the present representation. Do not start a different engine.

Evidence justifying this proposal
The +0.3 mm full split has 324 scalar degrees of freedom but rank 288. A continuous deformation mode exists entirely between the material samples and is invisible to both their position and strain checks. A saved-data Gauss integration diagnostic restores full rank with the same geometry, density and total mass. It changes base numerical inertia by 6.105% and one saved state's energy by 0.2003%, so it needs fresh-state validation and cannot inherit exact old discrete-energy preservation claims.

Proposed change and retained contract
Integrate mass and elastic energy over the reference material cells and cut-created subcells, rather than assuming the original particle locations resolve every new basis function. Particle locations may remain visualization/observation locations, but mechanical integration must resolve the full displacement space. Use a single explicit definition of displacement/strain, mass and energy throughout the fresh run. Use exact polynomial mass integration where justified; establish energy integration convergence separately because neo-Hookean energy is not polynomial. No added mass floor, deleted material, artificial rest reset or softening.
Preserve the body exterior, density/total mass, E=100 kPa, nu=0.4, Gc=80 J/m2, physical stops, prior results and existing Isaac/DiSECt setup. This changes numerical integration, not the material law. No contact or physical fracture is implied. The current single-plane/small-block proof remains the first scope; arbitrary oriented and intersecting cuts are still separate work.

Bounded execution
One focused two-hour integration/conditioning proof; any dynamic pilot <=600 wall seconds. Stop at the first substantive unresolved rank, conservation, state-transfer or refinement failure. No full tomato, video or training. The two-hour budget is an effort limit, not a promise of a working arbitrary-cut tomato.

1. Freeze v05; create v06 with the revised integration contract before running dynamics. Compute mass, center of mass and second moments against independent box integrals, and verify force as the energy gradient.
2. Compare intact original/candidate quadrature and document the new accuracy reference. Check rest, rigid motion, affine strain, nonuniform load/unload and halved timestep from fresh states. Do not silently carry a loaded old state into a differently integrated energy functional.
3. Test the aligned plane, the demonstrated +0.3 mm failure, a few representative offsets in the same cell, and near-boundary positions. Report full mass rank and conditioning, true material volume and smallest supported feature. Do not assert cut-anywhere support from a few offsets; define the tested envelope and explicit failure/stop behavior for thinner remnants.
4. Introduce partial/full prescribed cuts during loaded motion. Preserve x/v/F and mass. Make the integration scheme/refinement precise enough to bound any apparent stored-energy change at basis insertion; no fracture payment may be credited to numerical quadrature error. If exact old discrete-energy preservation is impossible, report it and require a reviewed numerical tolerance rather than modifying the threshold after a result.
5. Repeat bridge, independent-fragment, rotation/translation, history/checkpoint and volume checks. Compare mass/force/energy convergence at increased integration order and half timestep, with the original mechanical screens unchanged.
6. Report complete measured CPU cost and memory before proposing GPU or long-run work. This repair addresses missing mechanics, not speed; it does not by itself remove the existing RL-throughput blocker. A reasonable formulation must then support a separate efficient implementation, rather than expanding this slow reference into days of demonstrations.

Exit
Pass only the new integration component within its measured envelope. A new physical blade cut still requires the contact and paid-fracture gates. If the short proof fails, preserve one concrete diagnosis and stop for a strategy decision; do not keep appending numerical repairs indefinitely.
