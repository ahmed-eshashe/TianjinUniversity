v05 RESTRICTED CUT-BASIS FORMULATION

Reference material points X carry constant volume Vp and mass mp=rho Vp. The original quadratic basis is N0. For one prescribed plane, exact knot insertion maps its coefficients into a larger discontinuous tensor basis Nfull by K. Additional modes E span the complement of K. Only transverse supports wholly inside the finite prescribed rectangle receive those additional modes. T=[K, eligible E], N=Nfull T. Original basis functions remain exactly present.

State q and generalized velocity vg define x=N q, v=N vg and F=sum_a q_a outer grad(N_a). Kinetic energy is 1/2 vg^T M vg with M=N^T diag(mp) N; strain energy is sum_p Vp psi(Fp). psi is the unchanged compressible neo-Hookean law used by v04. Internal force is the exact negative derivative of that discrete strain energy. External particle forces map through N^T. Supports use the consistent-mass constraint projection. Velocity Verlet advances q/vg with no recurring transfer filter or intentional damping.

Topology proposals are previews. Old coefficients are copied into the nested space, new coefficients are zero, and mass rank/conditioning plus x/v/F/energy preservation are checked before commit. The original particle masses and their reference positions never change. q has absolute original coefficients and displacement-like enrichment coefficients; its rigid-translation generator is [1 for original coefficients, 0 for enrichment]. This generator is used for support linear impulse. Event history is monotonic and second-plane capacity is explicitly rejected. Restart rebuilds the same validated history and then restores all existing numerical state; legacy snapshots lacking history are rejected.

The present topology commands are prescribed mathematical unit fixtures. There is no contact law, damage evolution, work-based fracture criterion or paid release. Gc is unchanged but unused. No tensile separation qualifies as physical cutting merely because a discontinuity was inserted. Full-piece translation/rotation is a manufactured independence control, not a demo animation or physical slice result.

Unsupported cases: off-grid sampling demonstrated at +0.3 mm; plane through a material sample (ambiguous one-sided sampling); second or intersecting planes; arbitrary plane normals; curved or arbitrary trimmed body boundaries; fragment collision; real CAD blade contact. Finite front activation is support-sized, not subcell area accounting. The current exact full-mass formulation is not asserted to be a fast RL backend.

Continuous volume checks in the aligned fixtures use the existing Bernstein derivative enclosure plus interval determinant on each side's tensor basis at shared sample times. The inherited engineering rounding allowance is not a formal interval-arithmetic proof. The dynamic update checks pointwise J every step. No claim of an every-substep whole-volume certificate is made.

Research context: exact knot insertion and continuity control are established spline ideas; see reports/sources.txt. The particular finite-support enrichment and its restrictions are this isolated implementation. No claim of reproducing a published full cutting engine is made.
