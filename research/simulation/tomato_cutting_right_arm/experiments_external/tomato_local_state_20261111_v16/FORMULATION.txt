V16 LOCAL PERSISTENT GRID STATE — APPROVED APPROXIMATION PILOT

This is a new particle-integrated, mass-lumped total-Lagrangian discretization. It uses local grid states inspired by persistent-history MPM, but is algebraically a trilinear nodal Galerkin method. It is NOT a reimplementation of BladeMaster's MUSL/PIC/FLIP transfers, NOT official CRESSim, and NOT the v14 enriched spline with its mass matrix lumped. These differences must remain visible in reports.

Before integration: nodes X_a, positive reference material-point quadrature V_p, trilinear N_pa and G_pa=grad N. Eight Gauss points per full cell. Sum N=1, sum G=0, affine completeness. m_a=sum_p rho V_p N_pa. No mass cutoff or renormalization that removes material. Nodal unknowns u_a,v_a persist in time. x_p=X_p+sum N_pa u_a, v_p=sum N_pa v_a, F_p=I+sum u_a outer G_pa. GPU forms differences from one corner to preserve rigid translation numerically.

U=sum V_p W(F_p), with existing compressible neo-Hookean W. f_a=-sum_p V_p P_p G_pa; external point forces use the adjoint N. T=0.5 sum_a m_a |v_a|^2 (the new declared lumped kinetic energy, not the old consistent kinetic energy). a=f/m. Velocity Verlet: v_half=v+dt a/2; u_new=u+dt v_half; a_new=f(u_new)/m; v_new=v_half+dt a_new/2. No transfer filter, damping, hidden reset, particle velocity independent of displacement, or independently accumulated F. This avoids the earlier unobserved-strain mode and zero-time transfer damping by construction; analytical/independent tests must verify it.

At each reference node, incident material-point histories induce compatibility edges. Within each OLD node group, connected components of compatible labels determine children; incompatible old groups NEVER merge. Zero/unvisited history may keep both sides tied locally until a resolved front passes, retaining the intact bridge. New child states copy the old nodal u/v exactly. Child masses are sums of the original positive quadrature contributions. Thus total mass, nodal momentum, lumped kinetic energy and each material point's x/F/U remain invariant at split. This is a different constraint-based compatibility model from the paper's pairwise momentum mixing. It has a finite resolution delay; do not call it arbitrary subcell fracture. Rank/hourglass, partial-bridge and independent-fragment controls are mandatory. A geometry-based split spends no fracture energy and is not physical cutting.

Float64 CPU reference and float32 GPU candidate are compared explicitly. The fresh approximation scope includes this declared arithmetic difference; no production precision changes occur. Timestep and spatial refinement determine accepted accuracy. Minimum qualified slice thickness starts at four grid cells; 50 um score accuracy is not inherited. Full-cell/field volume and whole-material knife penetration checks are prerequisites to physical qualification, not replaced by point-center distances.

First pilot: 12 mm block, intact force/energy/objectivity/mass/momentum, strain consistency, free dynamics/time refinement and cost. Next only on pass: local splits and persistence; 60 mm body cost including history. The four-hour scope cap is a decision limit, not a promise of a completed tomato.

Primary design reference: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27342v1 (persistent labels and local reference neighborhoods). The persistent nodal variational update above is our explicit design difference; no published speed is inherited.
