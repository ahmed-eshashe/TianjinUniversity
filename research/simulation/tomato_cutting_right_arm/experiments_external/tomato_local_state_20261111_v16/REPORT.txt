V16 LOCAL-STATE APPROXIMATION PILOT — NOT RL READY

Outcome
The approved isolated pilot was implemented and tested. Its early intact-mechanics checks pass, but angled cutting fails a necessary mechanical rank check. The fastest predeclared intact timestep gives 46.61 equivalent aggregate 50 Hz transitions/s in the eight-copy bulk-only diagnostic, below the 100/s entry target before contact, evolving topology, robot, reset or policy work. No full tomato or RL-ready engine was produced. No physical cutting simulation or training was started.

What changed in this isolated candidate
New trilinear reference-grid fields, positive row-sum local nodal mass, persistent nodal displacement/velocity, variational neo-Hookean forces, velocity Verlet, float64 CPU reference and float32 GPU implementation. Position and deformation gradient derive from the same state; there is no recurrent velocity-transfer filter. The constitutive parameters remain E=100 kPa, nu=0.4, rho=1000 kg/m^3. This is our particle-integrated nodal method, not official BladeMaster or CRESSim. Local inertia and spatial resolution differ from the v14 reference; no matched-accuracy speedup over v14 is claimed.

Verified components
- Analytical affine deformation/strain/energy, frame objectivity, internal force and torque balance, and finite-difference force/energy consistency passed. Energy-gradient relative discrepancy: 4.81e-8.
- CPU/GPU short-state comparison passed: maximum point-position discrepancy 2.8e-10 m, velocity 1.65e-8 m/s, F entries 8.04e-8.
- Intact, aligned full cut, aligned partial cut and one offset full cut have expected strain-operator rank. Their geometric node-split transfers preserved mass, momentum and mechanical state to reported roundoff. These are topology unit fixtures, not blade-created physical fracture.
- Intact release comparison at dt=25 and 12.5 microseconds over 20 ms: maximum point-position difference 1.55 micrometres, velocity 1.99 mm/s. Energy residual at 25 microseconds: 0.3303%, within the 5% screen. This does not qualify any cut/contact timestep.

Decisive mechanics failure
For normal [1,0.27,0.13] and offset 1 mm, the order-2 cut model has tangent rank 119 instead of 120. It contains an extra non-rigid deformation mode with no corresponding linear strain energy. Other angled cuts have two or six extra modes. Increasing to order-3 quadrature does not reliably fix it: the first case still has rank 133 instead of 135. The failed controls are retained in preflight.json and rank_sampling.json. Do not hide this with damping or advance it to physical tomato qualification.
