WHAT WAS TESTED
The 12 mm block retains v09's synthetic E=100 kPa, nu=0.4, rho=1000 kg/m^3,
1.728 g total mass, quadratic basis with 3 mm spacing, full consistent mass,
order-5 knot-cell quadrature and float64 GPU mechanics. There are 512 material
observations; these are not independent point masses. Primary dt=3.90625e-7 s;
comparison dt is half that value. Mechanical source files were copied unchanged.
The inherited Gc=80 J/m^2 is unused by this geometric cutting experiment.

The unmodified saved CAD asset has 74 vertices and 144 triangles. Its straight
lower perimeter segment is represented by the midpoints of vertex pairs (14,15)
and (16,17), approximately 104.5 mm apart. This finite centreline is a geometric
cut marker, not a replacement for full-thickness blade contact. The entire CAD
mesh is retained for a sufficient final clearance test.

The material field must currently pass a continuous coefficient-level affine
check. Old/new material maps pull the moving edge into reference coordinates;
only supported reference-axis rectangular sweeps can add coverage. Coverage is
the union of actual swept patches, never their bounding rectangle. A basis
support releases only when fully covered. Untouched gaps and partial bridges
remain connected. Reverse/held/along-edge motions cannot create new coverage.
Topology construction remains on CPU; the GPU advances material and is rebuilt
when connectivity changes. Failed proposals restore material, clocks and history.

The main trial translated the block on the GPU while an imposed blade marker
traversed it in fourteen 1 ms actions (1 mm each). The 1 m/s marker speed serves
this short geometry diagnostic only; it is not an accepted robot cutting speed.
Four topology updates occurred; the thirteenth action completed one geometric
interface and yielded two independent pieces. The initial and intermediate
interfaces were coincident: topology insertion did not inject a position gap.

LIMITS THAT PREVENT PHYSICAL CUTTING QUALIFICATION
1. No blade contact reaction, irreversible material-failure decision or paid
   fracture work is present. Geometric passage alone selects separation here.
   Near-miss/spine controls are geometric queries, not loaded blunt-contact tests.
2. The current inverse map deliberately rejects non-affine (uneven) deformation.
   An actual loaded example demonstrated that rejection and full rollback.
   Simply loosening the affine tolerance would risk cutting the wrong material.
3. Full-support release with h=3 mm caused first activation only after 3 mm of
   edge coverage inside the block. This does not demonstrate crack-front or
   contact resolution compatible with the retained 0.1 mm penetration limit.
4. Oblique/curved sweeps, rotating blades during an action and multiple unfinished
   incisions in one connected fragment remain unsupported. At most four cuts
   may start. The straight edge marker does not cover every knife perimeter part.
5. There was no tomato geometry, skin/flesh calibration, board, gravity, robot,
   fragment re-contact or real pickup in this test. Experimental validation and
   full Gates 2/3 remain pending. The 2 N and 0.1 mm contact limits were preserved
   for future tests, not exercised or passed by this no-contact experiment.
