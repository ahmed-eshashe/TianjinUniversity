ONE PROPOSED REPRESENTATION CHANGE — NOT ADOPTED OR QUALIFIED

Observed blocker
The mass-lumped trilinear local-state candidate preserves affine strain and energy/force consistency in intact controls, but exact tangent-rank tests find extra zero-strain modes after oblique cuts. More uniform quadrature does not remove every failure. This is a discretization defect, not a tomato material calibration issue. Do not add arbitrary damping or call it physical cutting.

Concrete proposed correction
Use linear tetrahedral local fields with virtual child nodes and explicitly clipped material domains, in a new isolated directory. This remains a local explicit continuum model, but it is a different spatial approximation: lumped virtual-node tetrahedral FEM, not official CRESSim, BladeMaster or the old DiSECt setup. Keep E=100 kPa, nu=0.4, rho=1000 kg/m^3 and the same compressible neo-Hookean law initially. No material softening or physical stop changes.

For each parent tetrahedron, retain its four linear barycentric basis functions N and constant gradients G. A child material region has exact clipped volume V and first moment V*Xc. Its displacement/velocity are interpolated from its four logical nodes; F=I+sum u outer G is constant within that region. Thus U=V*W(F) and force=-V*P*G are exact for that declared field, without a sampling nullspace. Row-sum mass is rho*V*N(Xc), positive inside the parent tetrahedron. This is still approximate inertia relative to a consistent mass model, and a lower-order spatial approximation relative to the rejected trilinear field.

During a geometric unit split, clip only the affected material domain. Partition volume and first moment; clone parent nodal u/v into child histories. With unchanged linear parent field, U, each field value, lumped T and momentum should remain invariant to numerical tolerance. Persistent compatible node groups retain an intact bridge; incompatible old groups never merge. Do not discard small pieces, introduce mass floors, or make precomputed whole-plane cuts pass as blade-generated fracture.

Why this is worth one bounded check
The static algebra diagnostic proposal_rank.json examines six aligned/oblique full/partial fixtures. All six have the expected tangent rank and component counts; volume errors are below 5e-14 relative. This is NOT a dynamics, cutting, speed or tomato pass. The smallest predicted linear stability limit among those fixtures is approximately 52 microseconds, and arbitrary near-vertex cuts may be worse. Small-region conditioning remains a specific stop risk.

Bounded validation if adopted
1. Freeze the new formulation and transfer, retain v16 failures. Implement CPU reference and GPU candidate in isolation. First hour: exact energy/force gradient, rigid invariance, rank, mass/momentum, partial bridge, fragment independence, and short measured speed. Use the same 0.1 mm position, 5% energy/work and force-comparison criteria where applicable; no inherited pass.
2. Choose dt from half-step field/force/energy comparison, not from desired speed. Check sliver translations/angles; do not fix them with unreviewed mass scaling or removing material.
3. Only if mechanics passes, measure 60 mm simple bodies with changing cut history at one/four/eight copies. Entry remains 100 aggregate 50 Hz transitions/s. Bulk-only throughput is a necessary ceiling diagnostic, not an RL pass.
4. Limit this correction to two focused hours or the first substantive failed decision gate. Preserve one diagnosis; do not automatically branch into another numerical method.
5. Full tomato/contact/physical fracture remains blocked until local mechanics, fragment independence and speed support it. Real blade work, whole-material penetration, 2 N stop, 0.1 mm penetration limit, resets and eventual full environment throughput remain mandatory. Experimental calibration remains pending.

Scope decision
Adopt this one separate lower-order representation for bounded validation, or stop backend development and retain the current reference for offline demonstrations. A promise to finish an RL-ready tomato is not supported yet. The existing robot, kitchen, grip, controllers, DiSECt code and previous evidence remain unchanged in either case.

This proposal follows the stop/review requirement of docs/rl/ASTRA_HIGH_TOMATO_MODEL_EXECUTION_PLAN_20261110.md ("At the first failed decision gate, preserve the evidence and report the concrete blocker and next scope decision") and the approved v15 pilot's failed-mechanics stop. It does not request permission for routine implementation steps.
