VISION-GUIDED CONTACT PICKUP — current staged approach
14 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai

User decision
Learn the visual grasp position and orientation first; use the tested motion
controller; learn the entire movement afterward. Keep real finger contact and
friction, with no fixed attachment. Preserve the original cutting simulation.

What is learned
A convolutional model predicts two handle landmarks from actual 320x240 RGB-D
images from the robot's head. A frozen camera calibration and known flat resting
height convert those image landmarks into a grasp position and yaw. The predictor
accepts images only: sampled knife position, orientation and episode identity do
not enter it. A fixed neutral start is independent of knife placement.

What is engineered
Inverse kinematics and a smooth joint-space approach, a short downward approach,
compliant finger closure, lifting, and outcome/safety checks. The controller
receives joint feedback; simulated contacts are used for guarded pickup checks.
This is not an end-to-end learned joint controller or an RL-trained motion policy.

Physical changes and rejected trials
- v01: learned targeting with the previous grip controller. At 960 Hz, two of
  three validation placements passed; one hit the closing-load stop.
- A 1920 Hz check avoided that closing spike but later slipped and dropped.
- v02: uniformly stronger holding effort was rejected because it slipped sooner.
- v03: unchanged initial closure; the holding effort is redistributed toward
  thumb/index and away from rear fingers to reduce twisting. A one-second ramp
  uses thumb .65, index .16, middle .07, ring .025, pinky .01 Nm. Actuator limits,
  friction, mass, geometry, blade-contact rule and all success/load limits remain
  unchanged. Physics 1920 Hz; command rate 20 Hz; load window 1/960 second.

Accepted test scope
Shortened 305 mm, 175 g knife; original handle; bounded shifted/rotated flat
placements. A three-second continuous unsupported hold is required, with <=10 mm
relative slip, <=15 degrees relative rotation, opposed thumb/finger contact,
whole-knife clearance, and no contact-load or blade-contact violation. The hold
can include the last part of lifting; it is not a separate three-second stationary
hold. Provisional friction/mass parameters are not physically calibrated.

Open scope
Other knife geometries, wider board/yaw coverage, different stable resting poses,
clutter/occlusion, missing or noisy sensing, and physical transfer need independent
qualification. These are not established by success on the initial curriculum.
The full-motion students and their failed outcomes remain preserved separately.

Later recovery work (preserved separately)
v03 did not pass its independent gate: two of four attempted test placements
passed; two closing-load stops ended the batch. Those inspected failures became
regression evidence, not independent tests for subsequent controller versions.
v05 introduced contact-guided finger-before-thumb closure; both old failures
passed, but one new validation slipped when lifting began.
v06 balances thumb/index loading while the board still supports the knife; one
validation stopped at a fixed closing deadline despite developing contact.
v07 waits for .35 s of loaded opposed contact (8 s maximum), balances the pinch
for 1.5 s while supported, then lifts. Frozen new tests are in
curriculum_recovery_v02.json. See CURRENT_CANDIDATE.json and the matching report.

Controller sensing disclosure
The learned network receives RGB-D only, plus fixed geometric calibration in its
target conversion. The engineered gripper uses simulated contact feedback for
closure readiness. Handle/blade classifications and full object state also serve
simulation safety/outcome checks. This is a simulation hybrid, not a hardware-ready
camera-only control stack; physical transfer needs an appropriate tactile or
proprioceptive contact observer and independent calibration.

Data convention
Native root_pose_w is the actor/link-frame pose; root_vel_w is COM-frame velocity
expressed in world coordinates. Pose-based clearance/slip calculations use the
link pose. Velocity logs must be interpreted using that COM convention.

Frozen-contract wording correction
The v07 contract retains an older 'transition' string saying that holding pressure
begins at lift. Its detailed 'closing_sequence' and actual frozen source correctly
start the ramp during the 1.5-second supported balancing phase BEFORE lift. This
documentation clarification does not alter the frozen source or evaluation.
