NEXT PHASE — broader head-vision pickup

Preserve hybrid_pickup_v07.py, its checkpoint and all results as the first limited
baseline. Its fresh test result is 7/8, not universal pickup qualification. The
original full image-to-joint student failures remain separate.

1. Fix physical coverage before expanding learned motion.
Use new development placements to address the observed dropped grip. Establish
teacher pickups for the wider handle and heavier blade. The wider-handle probe
retained contact but failed whole-tool clearance; the heavier blade stopped on
pre-lift grip readiness. Keep the same friction, mass, actuator and contact limits.
Do not count these inspected variant probes as future independent tests.

2. Expand perception beyond the current flat-height assumption.
Collect real head RGB-D after stable settling across supported board positions,
angles, knife geometry and stable resting faces. Include failed estimates as
rejections, not successful demonstrations. Learn/estimate grasp height, orientation
and handle dimensions needed by the controller rather than supplying object truth.
Keep any training labels and reset parameters out of deployed actor inputs.

3. Reserve genuinely new evaluation cases before tuning.
Keep geometry families, placement bins and resting-face combinations separate
from development. Include occlusion, lighting and missing/invalid depth probes.
Measure actual three-second unsupported retention, whole-tool clearance, drift,
blade contacts, load/joint stops and drops; report each failure explicitly.

4. Only then learn the full movement.
Record successful contact demonstrations from the broadened task. Learn bounded
motion corrections or imitation using images/proprioception; re-evaluate physically.
Do not treat a good landmark score or a replay as a successful learned pickup.
Large-scale RL throughput and transfer to a physical robot remain separate gates.

The original kitchen/cutting/DiSECt setup remains unchanged. Board centering and
head camera live in the isolated v32 scene. No attachment, suction, object drives
or post-reset knife pose commands may be used to conceal a grasp failure.
